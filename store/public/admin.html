Orders

Authorized means the customer's card is on hold but has not been charged yet — that's the normal state to review before you capture. Captured is paid. Recorded is a cash or check sale that never touched a card.

Record a sale

For feed, milk, cheese, or goat sales handled in person — cash or check. This does not touch Stripe; it just adds a line to the ledger.

Tax report

"To" is exclusive of that day itself — to cover a full month, set "To" to the 1st of the next month.

Inventory

Switch a product on or off, or update its price, cost, or freight when a supplier's numbers change. Leave a dollar box empty to use that product's regular default instead of changing it — that is different from entering 0.00, which means exactly zero. Margin is price minus cost minus freight; if a row turns red, it is priced below what it costs to sell. Replace a photo with the button under its thumbnail — the new picture only goes live when you press Save on that row.
