Orders

Authorized means the customer's card is on hold but has not been charged yet — that's the normal state to review before you press Charge card. Captured is paid. Recorded is a cash or check sale that never touched a card.

Record a sale

For feed, milk, cheese, or goat sales handled in person — cash or check. This does not touch Stripe; it just adds a line to the ledger.

Products

Everything you sell. Change a price, cost, on-hand count or the Active tick right in the row, then press Save (or Save all at the bottom). Open a product with its arrow for the photo, description and stock history. To count the shelves, press Count stock.

What do the columns mean?

Cost is the average of what your purchases actually cost you, shipping included. Margin is price less cost, and it turns red when something sells for less than it costs. On hand is a count: type what is on the shelf and save. Leave it empty for things you don't count, like milk.

Purchases

What you buy from suppliers. Type each item's SKU and press Enter to move across. Shipping is shared across the items by weight so each one shows what it really cost you. Once a purchase is received, your stock goes up and each product's cost is updated.

New invoice

For orders that come in by phone — totes, big orders, regulars. The customer gets an email with a Pay invoice button and pays by bank transfer or card on the store. If they pay cash or check at pickup instead, find the invoice on the Orders tab and press Paid cash/check. An invoice that has not been emailed is marked Not sent yet on the Orders tab, and shows in Needs attention if it stays that way.
